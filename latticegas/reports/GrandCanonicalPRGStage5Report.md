# Phenomenological-RG follow-up: two crossings, a scattering-scaling mismatch

## Outcome and claim boundaries

Two independent scale-factor-\(3/2\) **finite-volume Binder crossings** remain certified, conditional on ideal independent equilibrium samples and an external empirical-Bernstein theorem:

| size transformation | population crossing bracket | conditional confidence | quotient sampling parameter |
|---|---|---:|---|
| \(4\to6\) | \((12/19,11/17)\) | at least 95% | \(16/25\) |
| \(8\to12\) | \((7/11,20/31)\) | at least 95% | initially \(41/64\), Binder-refined once to \(34/53\) |

The joint crossing coverage is at least 90% by a union bound, under those assumptions. Neither bracket is an interval for the infinite-volume critical point. The sampled rational parameters are *approximations* to their pair's unknown exact crossing; no theorem equates them to it.

At the final sampled parameters, the effective thermal dimensions agree within the predeclared exploratory bootstrap uncertainty. The effective lowest-mode scattering dimensions do **not**: the larger-minus-smaller bootstrap difference interval is \([0.134,0.235]\), excluding zero. A one-shot Binder-only correction made the fresh \(8-12\) Binder difference small (\(+0.00154\), exploratory interval \([-0.00500,0.00866]\)), but the scattering discrepancy grew. Therefore the requested milestone of **compatible thermal and correlation/scattering dimensions is not achieved**. This negative result is retained, not tuned away.

## Assumption, proof, numerical, and statistical layers

- **Microscopic assumption:** finite periodic cubic zero-field Ising spins with rational Gibbs weight \(\pi_r(s)\propto r^{D(s)}\), \(0<r<1\). This grand-canonical branch is separate from the old canonical Kawasaki data.
- **Lean theorems:** the concrete six-neighbour heat-bath probability is the Gibbs conditional; its site kernel is detailed-balanced and stationary; monotone coalescence fixes all starts; \(d\mathbb E[f]/d\log r=\operatorname{Cov}(f,D)\); the Binder-slope formula and endpoint-sign-to-crossing logic follow. The new finite Fourier-power support theorem gives \(0\le S_{\rm spin}(k)\le L^3\) for each sample. `GrandCanonicalPRG.lean` builds and `leanchecker` plus `GrandCanonicalAudit.lean` report only Lean's standard `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` axioms. The ideal two-sided stationary reference construction used in the CFTP statement remains an explicit premise.
- **Executable/numerical checks:** C++ uses exact integer heat-bath thresholds. Selected streams independently replay in Python; all 294,000 recorded stage-four/five seeds are distinct. A split-versus-uninterrupted CFTP regression is byte-identical. These checks do not prove universal C++ refinement or random independence. Fourier powers are recomputed from integer plane sums as exact quadratic-algebraic quantities with outward rational enclosures; each sample is checked against \([0,L^3]\). The trigonometric lookup tables are not yet Lean-proved. Floating Fourier output and plots are diagnostics only.
- **Statistical assumptions:** CFTP's equilibrium and independent-replica claims require ideal iid random instructions. SplitMix64 makes runs replayable; it is *not* an iid theorem. Crossing signs and \(S\) intervals use [Maurer–Pontil empirical Bernstein](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~colt2009/papers/012.pdf), whose probability inequality is external to Lean. Lean verifies the deterministic consequence of the sign and slope premises, not that this PRNG satisfies them.
- **Heuristic/effective layer:** a finite-size scaling interpretation of the quotients is a tested effective hypothesis, not an exact consequence of the microscopic law. No \(T_c\), asymptotic \(\nu\), \(\eta\), or irrelevant exponent is fitted here.

The original certified \((7/11,20/31)\) bracket—already narrower than the repaired small-pair bracket—was kept. No failed \(9/14\) sign was promoted to a crossing theorem.

The recovery manifest checks every index and seed, the final fixed count, and both hashes. No partial-data statistic set the stopping count. ## Finite-size scaling measurements

Define \(y_t=\log(|U'_4(sL)|/|U'_4(L)|)/\log(3/2)\) and \(y_S=\log(S_{sL}(k_{\min})/S_L(k_{\min}))/\log(3/2)\), with \(U'_4=dU_4/d\log r\), \(s=3/2\), and each box's own \(k_{\min}=2\pi/L\). The covariance identity gives exact-rational *point* slopes. Spin \(S\) is averaged over the three lowest axis modes. For density \(n=(s+1)/2\), normalization by the mean grand-canonical particle number changes it by a common factor, leaving these size quotients unchanged.

| pair at sampled \(r\) | fresh draws per size | \(U_4(L)-U_4(sL)\) | \(y_t\) point (exploratory bootstrap 95%) | \(y_S\) point (exploratory bootstrap 95%) | \(Q_S\) conditional 95% familywise box |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| \(4\to6\), \(16/25\) | 30,000 | +0.00179 | 1.590 [1.501,1.679] | 1.749 [1.721,1.777] | [1.872,2.204] |
| \(8\to12\), \(34/53\) | 12,000 | +0.00154 | 1.575 [1.419,1.730] | 1.933 [1.891,1.978] | [1.559,3.015] |

The \(Q_S\) point ratios are about 2.032 and 2.190. The four individual \(S\) boxes have simultaneous conditional coverage at least 95% under iid exact sampling and the external concentration inequality; their two quotient boxes overlap. The logarithmic \(y_S\) displays use floating point, while exact-rational \(Q_S\) endpoints are the authoritative certificates. The exploratory 2,000-resample between-pair intervals are \([-0.193,0.159]\) for \(y_t(8,12)-y_t(4,6)\) and \([0.134,0.235]\) for \(y_S(8,12)-y_S(4,6)\). These bootstrap intervals have **no formal finite-sample coverage guarantee**. The conservative, separately predeclared twenty-observable empirical-Bernstein slope check leaves both slope quotients unresolved because every propagated slope interval crosses zero; it does not certify a thermal dimension. A narrow bootstrap interval must not be silently promoted to that stronger claim.

Thus the finite-size scattering exponent discrepancy persists after a Binder-only correction; its likely sources include strong finite-size corrections, remaining crossing-location error, and uncertainty-method limitations. The exact CFTP protocol removes a finite-burn equilibration explanation *conditional on ideal random instructions*, but not those other possibilities. Two small transformations do not determine an asymptotic scaling law.

## Scattering interpretation and provenance

With an **assumed** linear dielectric-density coupling \(\epsilon=\epsilon_0+a\delta\rho+O(\delta\rho^2)\) and weak scattering, \(I(q)\propto |a|^2S(q)\). The measured lowest-mode intensity therefore has the same size quotient as \(S\), subject to common probe factors. This compares different accessible wavevectors \(2\pi/L\), **not** a fixed wavelength; no visible-light opalescence is numerically predicted from these small boxes. The higher-level scattering claim remains conditional, while the unexpectedly strong long-wavelength fluctuations are directly measured.

## Reproduce and next question

Build the simulator with `mkdir -p scripts/build && g++ -O3 -std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Werror src/grandcanonical.cpp -o scripts/build/grandcanonical_prg_v4_resume`. Run `ELAN_HOME=$PWD/.elan .elan/bin/lake build GrandCanonicalPRG`, `ELAN_HOME=$PWD/.elan .elan/bin/lake env leanchecker GrandCanonicalPRG`, and `ELAN_HOME=$PWD/.elan .elan/bin/lake env lean lean/GrandCanonicalAudit.lean`. The exact sample manifests record the commands and seeds; the scripts under `scripts/` regenerate the trusted analysis artifacts from the recorded raw data. The stage-four interrupted original is intentionally not a valid 40,000-row dataset; use its recovered artifact and manifest.

The next scientific question is whether a larger independently certified scale transformation reduces the scattering-dimension discrepancy. That requires a new size pair and a tractable perfect sampler (potentially random-cluster CFTP if heat-bath CFTP becomes too expensive), not another adjustment of \(r\) on these two pairs. Until then, the strongest supported conclusion is two finite-volume crossings, an emergent size-dependent scattering enhancement, and a **falsified two-pair compatibility hypothesis** under the declared exploratory comparison—not asymptotic critical exponents or literal visible opalescence.
